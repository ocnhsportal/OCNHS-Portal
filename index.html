How to request a document

  1. Click Request a document and fill out the form.
  2. Keep the reference number.
  3. Check the status here in the portal.
  4. Claim it with a valid ID when the status says Ready for release. A representative needs an authorization letter and both IDs.

Good to know

  • The learner's permanent record (School Form 10, formerly Form 137) is usually sent school-to-school when the receiving school requests it.
  • The report card (School Form 9, formerly Form 138) is given to the learner at the end of the school year.
  • Processing time depends on the document.
